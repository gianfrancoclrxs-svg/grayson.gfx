How it works

  • Meeting first: a Zoom or Google Meet call is required before we start, for quality and smooth communication. You schedule it and create the link.
  • Payment: 30% downpayment, 70% balance on delivery. The balance can be paid in installments if you prefer.
  • Revisions: 5 included, extra revisions ₱200 each.
  • Graphics: Basic uses graphics you supply. Basic+ and up include custom-designed graphics.

Store publishing fees (apps)

  • Google Play: $25 one-time registration
  • Apple App Store: $99 per year
  • Year one for both: $124, then $99 per year
  • Fees are paid directly to Apple and Google under your own developer account. Advanced covers year one. Renewals and Firebase usage charges beyond the free quota are billed to you.

Each price covers one platform, such as a website or an Android app. Extra platforms, like an iOS version for the App Store, are quoted separately. For apps on Google Play, new personal developer accounts must run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days before going live, so plan for about two extra weeks.
